Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Inspicio reads your photos inside your browser. Your photos and their metadata are never uploaded, stored or seen by us.

Your photos

When you open a photo, your browser reads it on your own device. The photo, its metadata and its location stay on your device. Inspicio has no server that could receive them.

The site’s Content Security Policy also blocks the page from sending data anywhere, with one exception: the optional map described below.

Optional map

If a photo has a location, you can click “Show map”. Only then does your browser ask Mapbox for a map image. That request sends the photo’s latitude and longitude to Mapbox, together with what every web request includes: your IP address and basic browser details. Nothing is sent to Mapbox unless you click. Mapbox handles this data under its privacy policy.

The “Open in OpenStreetMap” link opens openstreetmap.org with the coordinates in the address. This happens only when you click it, and OpenStreetMap’s privacy policy applies there.

Hosting

Inspicio is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic browser details when it delivers the pages, and may keep them for a short time for security and to run its service. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Cloudflare may show us basic totals, such as the number of requests. We don’t use them to identify anyone.

No cookies, analytics or ads

Inspicio uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising. Your browser’s local storage keeps only which metadata sections you collapsed, so they stay collapsed next time. It never stores anything from your photos. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

Children

Inspicio is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don’t collect personal information through the site, there is usually nothing for us to show you or delete. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
